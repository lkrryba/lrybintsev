FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Who is this for?

Anyone applying for jobs with a CV, up to 3 pages. Reviews are tailored to the region you’re applying in, because UK, US, and EU conventions differ on length, photos, and personal details.

Why choose you?

I’ve reviewed hundreds of CVs, in hiring and in professional live streams, and I’ve been on both sides of the interview table many times. You get feedback based on how CVs are actually read.

What’s my experience?

Six years at Scrimba as an operations lead, developer, and hiring manager. Before that, a languages degree from King’s College London and a Linguistics MA with Merit from Goldsmiths, University of London. I’m also CELTA qualified, so explaining things clearly is a trained skill, not a claim.

What should you send?

Your CV as a PDF, up to 3 pages (email me first if yours is longer), plus one line on the role and region you’re targeting. PDF is what recruiters actually see, so that’s what I review.

When does the clock start?

When I receive your CV, not at payment. So if you pay on Monday but send your CV on Wednesday, a standard review is due Friday.

What’s your refund policy?

Reviews are personalised work, so I don’t offer refunds once delivered. If something in the review is unclear, email me and I’ll clarify at no charge.

What’s the founding rate?
