You Can't Stand at the Top of the Water Slide Forever.

I just got off a Google Meet call that was, objectively, a simple call. In my mind, it should not have been nerve-wracking. And yet.

Why was it nerve-wracking? Because it was someone I admire and had been wanting to talk to for a while. And I'm British. I always feel like I'm going to put my foot in it.

In my head before the call: What if I say something stupid? What if I ruin it?

I did it anyway.

You can't be the kid standing at the top of the water slide afraid to go down. You've got to go down the chute.

That's a Tina Fey quote (I only have a vague idea who Tina Fey is, but I know this quote, and it's a good one).

Sometimes you just have to do things. Just do them. Even when, especially when, your brain is telling you every possible reason why it might go wrong.

🎢
Go down the chute

That's it. That's the whole thought. Just do the thing you've been standing at the top of, working yourself up about.
