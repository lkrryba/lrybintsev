No One Misses Writing CSS by Hand. I Don't Care.

Occasionally I see people saying they really miss writing CSS by hand now that AI is doing it all. I don't accept that. And I'm going to tell you why.

No one is sad that AI writes CSS now, and I stand by that.

The claim keeps appearing: "Oh, I really miss writing CSS by hand now that AI is doing it all." Every time I see it, I have the same question.

What, exactly, do you miss?

Let's go through it.

⏳
Spending hours, days, even, figuring out why something isn't applying

Is that what you miss? Genuinely?

😤
Rage quitting because a missing full stop broke the whole site

One character. The entire site. Gone. You miss that?

📦
Centering a div

This one was basically a rite of passage. Not a fond memory.

Come on now.

No one misses writing CSS by hand. No one. And I don't care how many nostalgic LinkedIn posts say otherwise.
